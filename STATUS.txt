B4  Customer sign-up and data rules                                 NOT STARTED
    Sign-up and visit register as required by the Rajasthan cyber cafe rules and Indian data law, how long
    the data is kept and how it is deleted. The detailed spec is kept by the project owner and is given
    separately. Until B4 exists, customers have name, phone and PIN only, and the register is kept on paper.
    Decided 12 Nov: every register item is a setting (required / optional / off) filled in by the region
    chosen at setup; the owner can change all of them; a "check your local laws" message; warnings below a
    region's legal minimum. Minors: a child with own photo ID plays alone, without one needs an adult; a
    setting for one-time parent consent. Companion record on (India) with a switch. Retention chosen by the
    owner. Staff see all register data; every opening of an ID copy or photo is logged. Monthly report kept
    locally until the owner deletes it, with a yearly reminder. Also: set the web filter's query log to the
    chosen website-history period (India: 1 year).

OWNER DECISIONS (10 Nov 2026)
    In the code: rate card 30 min Rs 40, 1 hour Rs 60, 1.5 hours Rs 100, then Rs 40 per 30 min (extra time past
    bought time is also Rs 40 per 30 min); free if the customer leaves within 5 minutes; 30 min minimum; 30 min
    blocks with 5 free minutes after each; unused minutes (bought time and the unused part of a charged block)
    kept for 7 days, but nothing is kept from the free start; when time runs out the customer keeps playing and
    the extra time is billed ("lock the PC when time runs out" stays off). All PCs: same price, Windows 11 Pro.
    Also done: 3 wrong PIN tries lock for 10 minutes; only the owner resets a forgotten PIN (from his phone
    in B5.8); counter logins last until logout and the counter PC locks after 10 idle minutes; customer PIN
    4 digits; the owner's logo on the lock screen. Rules: the central IT (Guidelines for Cyber Cafe) Rules, 2011.
    Later: membership with discounts and perks.
