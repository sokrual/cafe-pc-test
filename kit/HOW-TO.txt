Cafe test v4 - 3 STEPS

1. Copy this folder to  C:\CafeTest
   On a PC that is free for 15 minutes, double-click START-TEST.
   - Click "Yes" when Windows asks for admin permission.
   - If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   - It takes about 10 minutes. It also checks the PC for harmful programs.
   - Then it asks to restart the PC. Click "Yes".

2. Use the PC normally for one or two days. Customers can play as usual.
   Nothing is blocked during the test. The test only writes down what it sees.
   - Please open every game on this PC at least once, and let it update if it asks.
   - Also open the mouse, keyboard and headset apps once.
   A small timer will show at the bottom-right. It hides by itself during games.
   Once in a while a short voice says "Gaming Cafe test message" in the headset. Nothing to do.

3. When done, double-click FINISH-TEST and answer the one question it asks.
   It puts the PC back exactly as before and makes a file "Cafe-Test-Report-....zip"
   in the C:\CafeTest folder. That folder opens by itself with the file selected.
   Send that file on Discord to the person who gave you this test.

(If FINISH-TEST is forgotten, the timer and voice switch off by themselves after 3 days.
 Running FINISH-TEST later still puts everything back.)

Once they confirm they got the file, you can delete the C:\CafeTest folder.
