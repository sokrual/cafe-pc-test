SHINZX TEST v3 - 3 STEPS

1. Copy this folder to  C:\ShinzxTest
   On a PC that is free for 5 minutes, double-click START-TEST.
   - Click "Yes" when Windows asks for admin permission.
   - If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   - It takes about 5 minutes, then asks to restart the PC. Click "Yes".

2. Use the PC normally for one full day. Customers can play as usual.
   A small timer will show at the bottom-right. It hides by itself during games.
   Once in a while a short voice says "Shinzx Gaming Hub test message" in the headset. Nothing to do.

3. Next day, double-click FINISH-TEST and answer the one question it asks.
   It puts the PC back exactly as before and makes a file "Shinzx-Test-Report-....zip"
   in the C:\ShinzxTest folder. That folder opens by itself with the file selected.
   Send that file on WhatsApp to the person who gave you this test.

(If FINISH-TEST is forgotten, the timer and voice switch off by themselves after 3 days.
 Running FINISH-TEST later still restores everything.)

Once they confirm they got the file, you can delete the C:\ShinzxTest folder.
